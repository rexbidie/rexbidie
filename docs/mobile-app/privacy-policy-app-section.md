# Privacy policy: additions for the app

**Status:** approved and added to `/privacy-policy` (November 2026). Apple and Google check
that the privacy policy covers the app before approving it.

Placement on the site: a new section **"Our Mobile App"** after section 6, plus the additions to

---

## Our mobile app

RexBid has an app for iPhone and Android. It uses the same account and services as rexbid.ie,
and this privacy policy applies to it in the same way. The app also uses the following.

**Push notifications.** If you allow notifications, your phone gives us a notification token
(a random identifier for the app on your phone, provided by Google Firebase Cloud Messaging and,
on iPhone, Apple's push service). We store it with your account so we can tell you when you're
outbid, when lots end, when you win, about payments, questions and your sales. We delete it
when you sign out or delete your account, or when it stops working. You can choose which
notifications you get in Profile → Notifications, or turn them all off in your phone's Settings.
Notifications are also kept in the app's Alerts list for 90 days.

**Face ID, Touch ID and fingerprint sign-in.** If you turn this on, your email and password are
stored only on your phone, in its secure storage (the iPhone Keychain or Android Keystore), and
unlocked by your phone's own face or fingerprint check. We never receive your face or fingerprint
data. You can turn this off at any time by signing in with your password and declining, or by
removing the app.

**Camera and photos.** The app asks for access to your camera and photos only when you choose to
add photos to a listing. Only the photos you pick are uploaded, as part of your listing.

**Staying signed in.** The app keeps you signed in on that phone (for up to 90 days without use)
so you don't miss notifications. On the website you can choose "Keep me signed in".

**No advertising tracking.** The app does not use advertising identifiers, Google Analytics or
the Meta Pixel, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

**Service providers for the app.** Google (Firebase Cloud Messaging) and Apple (Apple Push
Notification service) deliver notifications on our behalf. Payments in the app are handled by
Stripe, as on the website.

## Changes to existing sections

**Section 8 (Data Retention)**, add:

> If you delete your account, we remove your name, contact details, address, profile photo, saved
> cards, payout details, identity documents, watchlist, reminders, notifications and app sign-ins.
> We keep records of completed sales, payment requests, receipts, statements and payouts for six
> years for tax and accounting purposes. Questions and answers you posted stay on listings,
> shown as "Deleted user".

**Section 9 (Your Legal Rights)**, add:

> You can delete your account yourself at any time in Profile → Security → Delete my account,
> on the website or in the app, or see rexbid.ie/delete-account. If you have a live listing,
> a leading bid, an open offer or a sale in progress, this needs to finish first.

**Marketing section**, add:

> You can choose which emails you receive in Profile → Notifications. Emails about payments,
> auctions you've won, accepted offers, your sales and your account's security are always sent,
> because they are part of the service.
